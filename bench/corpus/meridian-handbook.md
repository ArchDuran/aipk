# Meridian Robotics — Company Handbook (internal)

Meridian Robotics was founded in 2019 in Tartu, Estonia, by Ingrid Kallas and Tomas Ruhe.
The company builds autonomous warehouse robots for cold-storage facilities.

## Key facts

- Headquarters: Tartu, Estonia. Secondary office: Gdansk, Poland (opened 2023).
- Employee count: 214 as of January 2026.
- CEO: Ingrid Kallas. CTO: Tomas Ruhe. VP of Engineering: Priya Nandakumar.
- The company motto is "Cold chain, warm logic".

## Product lines

Meridian ships two robot families:

1. **Frostline** — pallet-moving robots for freezer warehouses rated to −30 °C.
   Current generation: Frostline F4, released in October 2025.
2. **Icebreaker** — inventory-scanning drones for high-bay cold storage.
   Current generation: Icebreaker IB-2, released in March 2024.

The Frostline F4 uses the Meridian Motion Kernel version 7 (MMK-7) for path planning.
Every robot reports telemetry through the Meridian Grid protocol over a private 5G mesh.

## Support policy

- Standard warranty on all robots is 3 years.
- Battery packs are warranted separately for 18 months.
- Firmware updates ship on the second Tuesday of every month.
- Critical safety patches ship within 72 hours of confirmation.

## Certification

Frostline F4 holds CE marking and an ISO 3691-4 conformity assessment completed
in September 2025 by the notified body TUV Nord.
