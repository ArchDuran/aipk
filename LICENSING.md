# AIPK Licensing

AIPK's CLI and runtime are source-available under the [Business Source
License 1.1](LICENSE).

## Free use

AIPK may be used free of charge for:

- evaluation and non-production testing;
- local development;
- personal use;
- production use by an individual, or by an organization with fewer than
  10 employees and under $1,000,000 in annual revenue (the Additional Use
  Grant in the [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

## Commercial license required

A commercial license is required when an organization outside the
Additional Use Grant above uses AIPK in production. Examples:

- an internal employee-facing assistant at a company with 10+ employees;
- a legal, compliance, or support assistant used in day-to-day operations;
- an AIPK-powered feature shipped in a commercial product;
- a production deployment operated on behalf of a client.

## What counts as production?

Production use means a deployment that supports real business operations,
employees, customers, regulated processes, or commercial services — as
opposed to evaluation, development, or testing with non-real data.

## Design-partner pilots

Time-limited evaluation and design-partner pilots may be offered under
separate written terms regardless of organization size. See
[aipk.dev](https://aipk.dev) or contact us below.

## After the Change Date

On 2030-07-29 (or earlier per-version dates set in future releases), the
license for that version converts automatically to Apache License 2.0, as
specified in the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file. No action is required.

## Questions this document doesn't answer yet

Specific situations (group-of-companies headcount, contractors, revenue
calculation, integrator vs. end-client licensing, staging environments) are
resolved case by case until a formal policy is published. Ask before you
build on an assumption.
